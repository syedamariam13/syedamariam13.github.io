Project 08 · Final pre-production project

Skybound Courier

A young flying messenger races between falling islands to repair the ancient wind engines holding her city in the sky — and must decide whether its saboteur deserves defeat or understanding.

FormatMobile story-driven adventure game
RoleWriting · Story design · Art direction
PlatformPhone & tablet · Landscape
ToolsStoryboards · Flowcharts · AI prompting

Narrative built around motion.

Executive summary

The player controls Mira Windsong, a 14-year-old courier in the Skyreach Archipelago. When the wind engines begin failing, every level becomes both a rescue mission and a countdown. The story joins traversal, puzzle solving, and moral choice around one clean fantasy: ride the wind, mend a breaking world.

“In the floating city of Skyreach, messengers ride the wind itself.”

Story structure

The opening teaches glide and dash while Mira repairs Windmill Spire. The middle expands into Market Isle, Cloudmist Caverns, and the Sunken Engine Core, where new abilities uncover Kael’s history. At Storm’s Edge, the final challenge uses every learned mechanic and ends with a meaningful branch: fight Kael or reach the engineer he used to be.

Player fantasy & mechanics

Gliding, Wind Dash, double-dash, air-current control, falling platforms, wind puzzles, and short mobile-friendly levels all reinforce Mira’s identity. Story progression and ability progression are intentionally the same path.

Positive-change theme

The project asks whether broken systems should be destroyed or repaired, and whether listening can change an outcome. Its two endings turn empathy and consequence into playable story decisions.
